          Metric notes

          Macro-F1
          Unweighted mean of class-level F1 scores.
          Weighted-F1
          Class-level F1 weighted by support.
          Precision@10
          Relevant items in the first ten divided by ten.
          Recall@10
          Relevant items in the first ten divided by held-out relevant skills.
          NDCG@10
          Ranking quality with higher-ranked relevant skills weighted more.
